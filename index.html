Groove studio 808-inspired drum machine

One bar per pattern. Click: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5... Right-click or Delete clears a step. Patterns and sound settings save automatically in this browser. MIDI channel 10 triggers the internal drums.

Align a short reface sound against the drums by ear. Negative offset sends looper MIDI earlier; positive sends it later. Use headphones or wired audio for tighter timing. Web Audio does not provide direct ASIO access.

Audio uses the system default until another output is selected.
